Formboard VR · recorded on Meta Quest 3

Wire harness assembly training in VR, driven by KBL data

A real VW harness from its KBL file, flattened 1:1 onto a virtual formboard. You route it wire by wire with your bare hands, and every peg is checked. At the end you see where it sits in the car.

Try it in the browser or on Quest 3 Source on GitHub

The formboard

VW ignition harness, flattened from the 3D KBL geometry with every segment at its true length. The last wire snaps in, then the finished harness: 18 wires in their real colours, on pegs and Y-pegs that were placed automatically.

Routing a wire by hand

Pinch the wire end and pull it along the pegs. The wire grows to your fingertips. Each correct peg snaps green with a click; a wrong branch would flash red and count as an error.

In the car

The complete VW Old Beetle harness at 1:1, 183 segments from the KBL centre lines. Orange is the ignition harness you just built, in place at the engine.
