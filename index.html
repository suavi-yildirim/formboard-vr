Formboard VR · wire harness assembly training · demo videos

On Quest 3: press Enter VR. Pinch the glowing wire end between thumb and index finger and pull it through the pegs. Point at a panel and pinch to press a button. With controllers: hold the grip button, aim at the floor and release to teleport; left stick walks, right stick turns.
